#### exhibition-mode

This feature allows users with photovoltaic installations to create a public-facing "exhibition view" showcasing key performance indicators, real-time data and positive impact of their system. This mode is specifically designed to meet the requirements of publicly displaying funded projects, making it ideal for sharing on a television or any public screen.

- [Exhibition mode](https://docs.clever.gy/helpdesk/app/exhibition-mode.md): This feature allows users with photovoltaic installations to create a public-facing "exhibition view" showcasing key performance indicators, real-time data and positive impact of their system. #### global-vision

Global view provides a consolidated overview of all your supply points, allowing you to monitor aggregated energy consumption and production at a glance. This feature is designed for both companies managing multiple installations and residential users with more than one home.
